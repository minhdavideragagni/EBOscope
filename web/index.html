Source-grounded reconstructions

1 · Search

Find documented claims.

The system searches for criticism, limitations and assessment evidence around the named target. It reads retrievable page text, not search snippets alone.

2 · Reconstruct

Map the reasoning.

Mistral proposes target, assessor, purpose, criterion, indicator, evidence, grounds and qualification. Exact quotations are accepted only when they can be verified in the retrieved source text.

3 · EBO

Keep the judgement scoped.

The ontology separates an assessment from its resulting qualification. Bias categories classify a qualification; they do not replace its criterion or evidence.

EBOscope does not certify that a source is correct. It exposes how a documented bias claim is scoped and supported.
