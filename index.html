Go beyond “it is biased”

See what is behind a bias claim.

Enter a named artefact, process, dataset, map, model, or public work. EBOscope is designed to search for documented assessments and make their scope, criteria, evidence and qualifications explicit.

One task

From a bias label to an inspectable qualification.

01 · SearchFind sources.

Look for documented criticisms, limitations and assessments around a named target.

02 · ReconstructRecover the reasoning.

Separate target, assessor, purpose, criterion, indicator, evidence and grounds.

03 · QualifyKeep the claim scoped.

Represent a contextual epistemic-bias qualification instead of a global “biased / not biased” label.

04 · ReuseMake it machine-readable.

Let people and software inspect the same structured assessment history.

Why EBO?

Bias is more than a label.

EBO separates the activity of assessing from the qualification that results from it, preserving who assessed what and under which epistemic criterion.

Why AI?

AI helps read; EBO gives structure.

An LLM can propose mappings from text. The ontology supplies explicit semantic roles so the reconstruction is inspectable rather than just another generated explanation.

Why machine-readable?

Let context travel.

A structured qualification can be queried and reused without collapsing its scope, evidence or attribution into a decontextualized label.
